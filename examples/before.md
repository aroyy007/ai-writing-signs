In recent years, large language models have garnered significant attention, playing a pivotal role in natural language processing. However, their deployment on edge devices remains a crucial challenge. It's not just a memory problem — it's a latency problem.

To address this, we propose LiteQ, a novel and groundbreaking quantization framework that not only reduces memory but also enhances accuracy, paving the way for efficient on-device inference. Extensive experiments demonstrate that LiteQ achieves superior performance, reducing memory by 4.1× while improving accuracy by 0.8 points on MMLU, underscoring its effectiveness.

In conclusion, LiteQ serves as a testament to the power of careful quantization. Experts agree that such methods will shape the evolving landscape of on-device AI.
